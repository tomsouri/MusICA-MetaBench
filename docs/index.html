Bring Your Own Music: try the generated benchmark yourself

20 of the 300 multiple-choice items that MusICA-MetaBench generated from the ChoraleBricks dataset. Answer each one, then see the ground truth and what eight multimodal LLMs answered when given the same piece and the same question.

Something wrong with this item or with how a model's answer was evaluated? Report a problem with this item or email us.

Share your thoughts about the demo or the benchmark, or email us. This page stores and sends nothing; the links open a GitHub issue or your mail client.

Code and contribution instructions: github.com/tomsouri/MusICA-MetaBench. Preprint: arXiv:2607.06015.
